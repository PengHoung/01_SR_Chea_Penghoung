# Central University Library Guidelines

1. Borrowing Privileges:
Undergraduate students may borrow up to 5 physical books for a duration of 14 calendar days. Graduate students may borrow up to 10 books for 30 days.

2. Late Returns & Fines:
Overdue items incur a daily late fine of $0.50 per book. Unpaid balances exceeding $10 will temporarily suspend student course enrollment and graduation clearance.

3. Study Room Bookings:
Group study rooms must be reserved via the library mobile app at least 2 hours in advance. Reservations are capped at a maximum of 3 hours per study group per day.
