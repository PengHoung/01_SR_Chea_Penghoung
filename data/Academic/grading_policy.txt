# Academic Grading and Assignment Submission Policy

1. Late Submission Penalties:
Coursework submitted after the deadline receives an automatic penalty of 10% deduction per calendar day late[cite: 1]. Work submitted more than 3 days past the deadline will receive a grade of zero unless supported by an official medical certificate[cite: 1].

2. Plagiarism and AI Misuse:
Directly copying code or prose without citation constitutes academic misconduct[cite: 1, 8]. If an AI assistant is used for research, it must be explicitly cited in the bibliography.

3. Grade Appeals:
A student wishing to dispute a final exam mark must submit a written appeal form to the Academic Registrar within 7 working days following grade publication.
